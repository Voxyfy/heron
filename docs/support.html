Support

Write to haymana.batuhan@gmail.com. Include your macOS version and, for a failing snippet, the error Heron shows.

License

  • One key works on two Macs. To move it, open Settings ▸ License on the old Mac and click Release This Mac.
  • Lost the key? It is in your purchase email from Lemon Squeezy, and on your My Orders page.
  • Renewal is yearly. If you cancel, Heron keeps working until the paid year ends.

PHP not found

Heron looks for PHP from Laravel Herd, Homebrew (/opt/homebrew/bin/php) and /usr/bin/php. Point it at another one in Settings ▸ General.

Servers

Heron signs in with a password or an OpenSSH key (ed25519 or RSA). Newer servers refuse RSA signatures made with SHA-1; use an ed25519 key. The server needs php with the tokenizer extension on its PATH, or set the full path in the connection.
